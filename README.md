### Cosmology 🔭

As an undergraduate, I studied astrophysics at Cambridge, with a particular interest in cosmology which is the study of the Universe and everything in it. More broadly, I am also passionate about astrophysics and theoretical physics and have found that cosmology is the perfect marriage of the two.

While at Cambridge, I undertook a research project extending the standard internal linear combination method of separating the primary cosmic microwave background (CMB) anisotropy from its secondary anisotropies and astrophysical foregrounds. The approach I used was incorporating maps tracing large-scale structure (LSS) that I created from the _unWISE_ galaxy catalogue as additional inputs to the standard procedure. See A. Kusiak et al. 2023 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.08121) for an explanation of the method. I performed tests on data from the _Planck_ satellite and the Atacama Cosmology Telescope.

I am currently looking for PhD positions to work on analysing data from CMB and LSS experiments such as the Simons observatory, LiteBIRD, Euclid, DESI, and LSST to study the primordial Universe and fundamental physics.
